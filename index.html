A shared-market deck-building game

Build your deck, court the Legends, and race for Prestige. Play solo with four Chronicles, or choose two each with a friend.

Choose exactly four Chronicles.

How to play

Play cards from your hand to gain Grendels and Power. Spend Grendels to recruit cards from the Crossroads. Spend Power to defeat enemy Champions; leftover Power becomes Prestige when your turn ends.

Actions go directly to your Rest pile after resolving. Only Champions and Guards stay on the field.

After use, one-time-use cards rest until turn end, then return to the Crossroads stock. They cannot be drawn again that turn.

The Common Purse is always available in the Hall: pay 2 Grendels to replace a Copper in your Rest pile with a Silver worth 2 Grendels. It uses your Legend invocation and has no Allegiance dial.

You may invoke one Legend each turn. A Legend facing you grants Allegiance. All four Legends facing you wins. At 40 Prestige, your opponent gets a response turn: if only one player has 40 or more, the higher score wins. If both have 40 or more, the final target becomes 80. At 80, allow a response turn and compare scores; an exact tie is a draw. The target never increases beyond 80.

How the story unfolds

  1. Play cards to generate Grendels, Power, and special effects.
  2. Recruit cards from the shared Crossroads with Grendels. They enter your Rest pile.
  3. Damage rival Champions with Power. Damage persists; red health numbers show wounded Champions. Guards must fall first.
  4. Champion Effects resolve automatically on placement. On later turns, click each Champion to use its Effect once.
  5. Crimson and Midnight combos: Combo unlocks on your second same-suit activation that turn; Combo 2 on your third, and Combo 3 on your fourth. Only the card being activated receives its printed bonuses—earlier cards never trigger again.
  6. Golden Deceiver Paid Combos: after the eligible regular Combo resolves, choose whether to pay the printed price. The full card remains visible while deciding. Golden Opportunity is the exception to normal tier replacement: its Combo 2 is additional, so both discounts apply.
  7. Law in Effect plays immediately when purchased, then returns to the Crossroads stock instead of joining your deck. Its Combo 2 makes the opponent choose a card to discard at the start of their next turn.
  8. Invoke one Legend per turn if you can pay its cost.
  9. End your turn. Leftover Power becomes Prestige and you draw five new cards.

At 40 Prestige, allow the opponent a response turn. If both players reach 40, continue to a final target of 80. At 80, the higher score after the response wins; equal scores draw. Controlling all four Legends also wins.

The always-neutral Common Purse lets you pay 2 Grendels and exchange a Copper in your Rest pile for a Silver worth 2 Grendels. This uses your Legend invocation. It does not count toward the four-dial victory.

Gorgon's Curse: Petrify 1 creates one new Petrified Villager in your opponent's Rest pile without replacing a card. Villagers cannot be played. At the start of your turn, move Villagers from your hand to Rest without drawing replacements. A Villager drawn mid-turn stays unplayable and goes to Rest during normal end-turn cleanup.

Medusa removes all Petrified Villagers from your Rest pile and grants 1 Power per 2 removed, rounded down. Pay 4 Grendels when neutral, 3 when favoring you, or 5 when favoring your opponent. This uses your Legend invocation and moves the dial one step toward you. You may invoke with an empty Rest pile, but gain no Power.

Vaelis lets you secretly choose Heads or Tails for your next coin flip that turn. Pay 3 Grendels when neutral, 2 when favoring you, or 4 when favoring your opponent. An unused choice expires at the end of the turn.
